What App Lock Pro does with your data

LAST UPDATED — 23 SEPTEMBER 2026

App Lock Pro locks individual apps behind a PIN or pattern, and hides private photos, videos, files, and folders in an on-device encrypted vault called SafeBox. This page explains, plainly, what the app touches and what it never does.

In short
  • Your PIN, pattern, and SafeBox contents never leave your device. We have no server that stores or can see them.
  • No account, login, email, or phone number is required to use the app.
  • The app is free and shows ads — that's the one place limited data sharing happens, detailed below.

Permissions, and why each one exists

Every permission below maps to a specific feature. If the app doesn't have your permission for something, that feature just doesn't work — it doesn't ask again through the back door.

Usage Access
Lets the app notice when a locked app is opened, so it can show the unlock screen. Runs entirely on-device — the list of apps you use is never sent anywhere.
Display over other apps
Draws the PIN or pattern screen on top of an app you've locked, the instant it opens.
Camera
Used only for the optional "capture intruder photo" feature — a front-camera photo taken if someone enters the wrong PIN. It's saved to your local logs and never uploaded.
Photos, videos & files
Used only when you actively hide something into SafeBox, or restore it. Hidden items move into the app's private, encrypted storage — nothing is scanned in the background or uploaded.
Notifications
Shows status notifications from the app itself — for instance, that the lock service is active.
Run at startup
Keeps app-locking active after you restart your phone, and keeps Android from killing the lock-monitoring service while you're using your device.

Advertising

The app is free, supported by ads served through Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, and Unity Ads. These networks may collect an advertising identifier and general device information to show and measure ads, under their own policies:

Google — AdMob ↗ Meta — Audience Network ↗ Unity — Unity Ads ↗

Where your region requires it, the app asks for consent before showing personalized ads.

Purchases, notifications & analytics

If you buy an ad-free or premium upgrade, Google Play Billing handles the transaction end to end — we never see your payment details.

The app uses Firebase (Google) to deliver push notifications and basic, non-personal usage analytics such as crash reports, under Firebase's privacy policy ↗. None of this is linked to the contents of your locked apps or SafeBox vault.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We don't upload, back up, or have any remote access to your PIN, pattern, or SafeBox contents.
  • We don't sell your personal data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored locally — your SafeBox vault, PIN or pattern, and logs included. There's no separate server-side account to delete, because there's no account.

Children's privacy

App Lock Pro isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised version.
